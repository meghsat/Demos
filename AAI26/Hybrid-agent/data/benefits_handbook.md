## Section 2: Health & Wellness

### 2.1 Medical Insurance

**Plan Options**:
- **PPO (Preferred Provider Organization)**
  - Deductible: $250 (individual) / $500 (family)
  - Coverage: 90% after deductible
  - Out-of-network: 70% coverage
  - Monthly Premium: $350 (employee) / $850 (family)
  
- **HMO (Health Maintenance Organization)**
  - Deductible: $100 (individual) / $200 (family)
  - Coverage: 95% after deductible
  - Requires referrals for specialists
  - Monthly Premium: $280 (employee) / $720 (family)
  
- **HDHP (High Deductible Health Plan) with HSA**
  - Deductible: $1,500 (individual) / $3,000 (family)
  - Coverage: 100% after deductible
  - HSA-eligible (company contributes $500/year)
  - Monthly Premium: $180 (employee) / $480 (family)

**Company Contribution**:
- Employee-only coverage: 90% of premium
- Employee + family coverage: 70% of premium

**Eligibility**: Day 1 of employment

### 2.2 Dental Insurance
- PPO plan only
- Deductible: $50/year
- Coverage: 100% preventive, 80% basic, 50% major
- Monthly Premium: $45 (employee) / $120 (family)
- Company pays: 90% of employee premium

### 2.3 Vision Insurance
- Annual eye exam: 100% covered
- Frames/contacts: $150 allowance every 2 years
- Monthly Premium: $12 (employee) / $28 (family)
- Company pays: 100% of employee premium

### 2.4 Mental Health Support
- EAP (Employee Assistance Program): 6 free sessions/year
- Therapy coverage: Included in medical plans
- Crisis hotline: 24/7 access

### 2.5 Gym Membership Reimbursement
- Up to $75/month
- Submit receipts via Expensify

## Section 3: Time Off

### 3.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
- **Accrual Rate**:
  - Year 1: 15 days/year (1.25 days/month)
  - Year 2-3: 20 days/year (1.67 days/month)
  - Year 4+: 25 days/year (2.08 days/month)
- **Rollover**: Up to 5 days to next year
- **Payout on termination**: Yes, for accrued unused days

### 3.2 Sick Leave
- Unlimited sick days (honor system)
- Request via HR portal
- Doctor's note required for 3+ consecutive days

### 3.3 Bereavement Leave
- 5 days paid (immediate family)
- 2 days paid (extended family)

### 3.4 Jury Duty
- Fully paid
- No limit

## Section 4: Parental Leave

### 4.1 Primary Caregiver Leave
- **Duration**: 16 weeks fully paid
- **Eligibility**: Birth parent or primary adoptive parent
- **Flexibility**:
  - Can start anytime within 12 months of birth/adoption
  - Can be taken consecutively or split into blocks (min 2-week blocks)
  - Job protection guaranteed (FMLA compliance)

### 4.2 Secondary Caregiver Leave
- **Duration**: 6 weeks fully paid
- **Eligibility**: Non-birth parent or secondary adoptive parent
- **Same flexibility** as primary caregiver

### 4.3 Parenting Support
- **Childcare stipend**: $500/month for first year
- **Lactation support**:
  - Private nursing rooms (all offices)
  - Breast pump coverage (100% via medical insurance)
- **Part-time return option**:
  - 50-75% schedule for up to 3 months post-leave
  - Pro-rated salary
- **Parenting resource program**: Access to parenting counselors

**How to Apply**:
1. Notify HR 2 weeks before leave start (if possible)
2. Complete leave request form (HR portal)
3. Submit birth certificate or adoption papers within 30 days

(Source: Section 4.2 - Parental Leave)

## Section 5: Financial Benefits

### 5.1 401(k) Retirement Plan
- **Employer Match**: 50% up to 6% of salary
  - Example: Contribute 6% → Company adds 3%
- **Vesting**: Immediate (100% from day 1)
- **Eligibility**: After 90 days of employment
- **Provider**: Fidelity

### 5.2 Equity Compensation
- **Stock Options**: All full-time employees eligible
- **Vesting Schedule**: 4 years (1-year cliff, monthly vesting)
- **Strike Price**: Fair market value at grant date
- **Exercise Window**: 90 days post-termination (standard)

### 5.3 Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)
- Available after 1 year of employment
- Purchase company stock at 15% discount
- Payroll deduction (up to 15% of salary)
- 6-month offering periods

### 5.4 Commuter Benefits
- Pre-tax deduction for public transit
- Up to $300/month (IRS limit)

### 5.5 Professional Development Budget
- $2,000/year per employee
- Use for: Courses, conferences, certifications, books
- Approval: Manager + HR
- Reimbursement within 30 days

## Section 7: Work Arrangements

### 7.1 Remote Work Policy
- **Hybrid model**: 3 days office / 2 days remote (flexible)
- **Full remote**: Available for certain roles (manager approval)
- **Equipment**: Company provides laptop, monitor, accessories
- **Home office stipend**: $500 one-time setup

### 7.2 Flexible Hours
- **Core hours**: 10 AM - 3 PM (must be available)
- **Flexible start/end**: Arrive 7-10 AM, leave 3-7 PM
- **Total**: 40 hours/week average

### 7.3 Sabbatical
- **Eligibility**: After 5 years of continuous employment
- **Duration**: 4 weeks paid
- **Frequency**: Every 5 years
- **Purpose**: Rest, travel, personal projects
- **Job protection**: Guaranteed return to same role
